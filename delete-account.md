This page explains how to delete your game account and its data in games developed and published by **Cem Yasan**. You don't need to have the game installed to ask for deletion.

## Option 1: Delete in the game

1. Open the game and go to **Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete Account** and confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted right away.

## Option 2: Ask by email

If you no longer have the game installed, or can't open it, email **[yasancem@outlook.com](mailto:yasancem@outlook.com?subject=Account%20Deletion%20Request)** with the subject **"Account Deletion Request"** and include:

* The name of the game
* Your **Player ID**, if you can still open the game: it's shown at the bottom of the **Settings** screen, and tapping it copies it. If you email from the game's **Contact** button in Settings, your Player ID is added to the email for you.
* Your player name, as shown in the game's profile or on the leaderboards
* The platform (Android or iOS) and, if you signed in, whether you used Google Play Games or Apple

Your Player ID is the fastest way for me to find your account. Without it, I'll use your player name and the other details, which can take longer.

I'll confirm by email once your account is deleted, within **30 days** of your request.

---

## What is deleted

* Your game account and its player ID
* Your cloud save data (game progress, virtual currency, and unlocked items)
* Your leaderboard entries, including your player name and scores
* The link between your game account and your Google Play Games or Apple sign-in

## What is not deleted

* **Progress saved on your device.** This stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
* **Purchase records.** Purchases are processed and kept by Google Play or the App Store under their own policies. Deleting your account doesn't refund purchases.
* **Analytics and advertising data.** This data is tied to device identifiers rather than to your game account, so it isn't removed with your account. Our analytics and advertising partners keep it for a limited period under their own retention policies. See the **[Privacy Policy](/mobile-privacy-policy)** for details and for how to reset your advertising ID.

If you signed in with Apple, you can also stop using Sign in with Apple for the game in your iPhone's **Settings → [your name] → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple**.
