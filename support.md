Need help with one of my games?

## Contact

Email **[yasancem@outlook.com](mailto:yasancem@outlook.com?subject=Game%20Support)** and I'll get back to you as soon as I can, usually within a few business days.

You can also open the game's **Settings** and tap **Contact**, which starts an email for you.

To help me sort out your issue faster, please include:

* The game's name and version (shown in Settings)
* Your device model and OS version (for example, iPhone 15, iOS 27)
* What happened and the steps that led to it
* A screenshot or screen recording, if you can

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I bought something but didn't receive it
Close and reopen the game while connected to the internet. Purchases still waiting to be delivered are completed when the game starts. If the item still doesn't appear, email me with the date of the purchase and the receipt from Apple or Google. Never send me card or bank details.

### How do I restore my purchases on a new device?
Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you made the purchase with, open the game's **Settings** and tap **Restore Purchases**. Permanent purchases, such as removing ads or unlocking content, come back. Consumable items, such as in-game currency, can't be restored.

### How do I get a refund?
All payments are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them:

* **App Store:** [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
* **Google Play:** [Google Play refund help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637)

### I lost my progress
Progress is saved on your device, so uninstalling the game erases it. If the game lets you sign in, your progress is also backed up to your account, and signing in with the same account on a new device brings it back.

### I paid to remove ads but I still see ads
Removing ads turns off banners and ads that interrupt play. Optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for a reward, stay available. If you see interrupting ads after buying, tap **Restore Purchases** in Settings, and email me if they don't stop.

### How do I change my ad tracking or privacy choice?
* **iOS:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, then turn the game on or off.
* **In the game:** if a privacy or ad consent option appears in Settings, it opens your ad consent choices.
